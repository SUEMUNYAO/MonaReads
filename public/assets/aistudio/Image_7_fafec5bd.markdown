## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy of an independent boutique bookseller married to the precision and scale of modern literary commerce. It blends editorial poise—reminiscent of bespoke art quarterlies and classic Penguin paperbacks—with structured, trustworthy digital merchandising.

### Core Philosophy
- **Tactile Warmth over Sterile Utility:** Surfaces borrow the soft, unbleached quality of archival paper stocks, avoiding stark optical whites to minimize eye fatigue during extended browsing sessions.
- **Editorial Authority:** Merchandising presents curation as an art form. Typography, spacing, and micro-copy convey deep literary respect rather than algorithmic churning.
- **Translucent Commerce:** Clear pricing, format tabs (Hardcover, Paperback, Audiobook, e-Book), provenance indicators (Indie Bestseller, Staff Pick), and effortless checkout mechanics ground editorial romance in pragmatic e-commerce reliability.

### Design Movement
**Curated Modern Editorial.** A calibrated synthesis of literary classicism (disciplined serifs, rule dividers, crisp editorial frames) and modern minimalism (generous negative space, precise typography scale, low-contrast ghost borders, deliberate accent hits).

## Colors

The palette reproduces the sensory qualities of physical bookbinding: archival cream paper stocks, rich midnight ink, oxidized terracotta leather bindings, and muted botanical bookcloth.

### Functional Roles
- **Midnight Ink (`#0F172A` - Primary):** Serves as the high-contrast structural anchor for primary headings, body copy, key action states, and dense footer sections. Evokes hot-metal typesetting ink.
- **Warm Terracotta (`#C2410C` - Secondary Accent):** Reserved for intentional conversion moments—Add to Bag actions, curated staff ribbons, callouts, and limited-edition badges. Provides high-visibility warmth without synthetic aggression.
- **Refined Olive Sage (`#3F6212` - Tertiary Accent):** Applied to reading statuses, literary prize tags (e.g., Booker Prize, National Book Award), verified review badges, and sustainable shipping indicators.
- **Paper Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Base Neutral):** The universal canvas. Replaces blinding default white with unbleached laid paper.
- **Deckle Cream (`#F5F1EA` - Neutral Darker / Surface Tint):** Applied to secondary cards, comparative tables, format selectors, and dropdown menus to create soft, un-shadowed layer separation.
- **Book Edge Gray (`#E2DCD5` / `#CBD5E1` - Borders & Dividers):** Crisp, subtle line-work reinforcing publication-grade layout grids without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear structural tension between classical literary authority and effortless contemporary utility.

- **Headlines & Book Titles (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serifs provide instant editorial gravitas. Display sizes use subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to bind headline rhythm tightly.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its open apertures, modern geometric clarity, and legibility at smaller operational scales (specs, cart ledgers, ISBN codes).
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`):** Formatted in uppercase with `0.08em` tracking for shelf tags, category slugs, author surnames in lists, and binding formats.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural framework adheres to a refined broadsheet grid with disciplined horizontal baselines.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max container width capped at `1360px` to maintain comfortable eye-scan arcs across book jacket arrays and longform reviews.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Vertical Rhythms & Pacing
- **Editorial Breathing Room:** Section divides leverage `space-xl` combined with hair-line borders (`1px solid #E2DCD5`) rather than heavy background switches.
- **Book Display Grids:** Merchandising carousels and lists align book jackets along a shared baseline, fixing jacket heights to preserve crisp horizontal horizon lines across uneven cover dimensions.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from aggressive Drop Shadows and Neomorphic blurs, opting instead for **tactile planar depth, crisp low-contrast outlines, and delicate ambient paper lift**.

### Principles
- **Low-Contrast Deckle Borders:** Flat planes and cards sit on `#FAF8F5` defined by `1px solid #E2DCD5`. Surface separation is achieved by tonal contrast (`#FAF8F5` over `#F5F1EA`) rather than harsh elevation.
- **Book Spine Ambient Shadow:** Covers and book physical representations feature a bespoke dual-layer shelf shadow:
  - Ambient: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
  - Edge spine: `-2px 0 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`
- **Interactive Hover Drift:** Hovered product cards elevate by `translateY(-3px)` accompanied by an expanded warm ambient blur: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.09)`.
- **Overlays & Modals (Cart Drawer & Quick Look):** Backdrops utilize a muted ink scrim (`#0F172A` at 40% opacity) paired with a high-depth ambient cutoff: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes architectural precision, echoing physical book trimming, cloth-bound volumes, and bespoke bookplates.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Base Components (Level 1 - Soft / `0.25rem`):** Buttons, form inputs, book cover thumbnails, and interactive cards feature subtle `0.25rem` (4px) radii. This eliminates clinical digital sharpness while preserving the architectural dignity of printed material.
- **Containers & Drawers (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem`):** Flyout carts, modal panels, filter sheets, and dropdown panels use an understated 8px radius.
- **Badges & Format Pills (Intentional Exception):** Category tags, bestseller rosettes, and format selector chips (e.g., "Hardcover", "Audible") may utilize pill geometry (`9999px`) to create distinction between clickable filter tags and rectilinear product components.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag / Proceed to Checkout):** Solid terracotta `#C2410C` background, `#FFFFFF` bold label text, `0.25rem` radius, zero shadow. Hover state scales down brightness to `#9A3412`. Active state triggers a subtle inset down-shift.
- **Secondary Action (Browse Catalog / Quick View):** `#0F172A` border (`1px solid`), transparent surface, `#0F172A` text. Hover fills with `#0F172A` and switches text to `#FAF8F5`.
- **Tertiary / Literary Link:** Plain text with `border-bottom: 1px solid #C2410C` spanning only the text width, hovering with `color: #C2410C`.

### Book & Editorial Cards
- **Product Display Card:** Set against `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F5`, framed with `1px solid #E2DCD5`. Contains:
  1. Book cover image wrapped in spine shadow with format pill anchored top-right.
  2. Author name in `label-caps` (`#475569`).
  3. Book title in `title-book` (`#0F172A`).
  4. Curated review snippet or 5-star rating unit (olive-filled star icons `#3F6212` + count).
  5. Crisp pricing unit (`price-lg`) with strikethrough MSRP where applicable.
- **Staff Recommendation Card:** Incorporates an unbleached `#F5F1EA` paper container, a quotation mark in `Playfair Display`, and an editor's handwritten sign-off lockup.

### Format Chips & Badges
- **Format Selectors:** Multi-segment switchers for "Hardcover ($28.00)", "Paperback ($18.00)", "E-Book ($12.99)". Inactive: `#FAF8F5` surface with `#E2DCD5` border. Active: `#0F172A` surface with crisp `#FAF8F5` typography.
- **Curated Badges:**
  - *Bestseller / Staff Pick:* Terracotta tint (`#FFF7ED`) with `#C2410C` border and typography.
  - *Award Winner / First Edition:* Sage tint (`#F7FEE7`) with `#3F6212` border and typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs (Search & Checkout):** Built with `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`. Active focus produces a sharp `#0F172A` border with zero colored halo rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square and circular glyphs filled with `#0F172A` upon selection, designed with custom thin SVG checks to mimic archival ledger forms.

### Trust & Checkout Elements
- **Review Summary Block:** Aggregate review scores highlighted with solid geometric stars in deep sage (`#3F6212`), matched with a clear breakdown bar chart using terracotta progress indicators.
- **Order Ledger (Mini-Cart):** Clean editorial invoice style with thin dividers, concise typography, clear line-item format labels, and an upfront shipping/tax transparency table before the final primary CTA.
